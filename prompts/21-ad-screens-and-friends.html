Brand Advertising Revenue Model

Brands pay per-impression (CPM) for room themes and card backs. Players earn the brand skin free via a short challenge ("Play 3 games in ACME room"). Brand gets 1,000-10,000 impressions per campaign. Estimated CPM: $15-25 (US), $3-8 (India). Premium positioning because it's opt-in and non-interruptive.
